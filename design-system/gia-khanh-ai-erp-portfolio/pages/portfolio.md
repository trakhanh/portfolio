# Portfolio Page Override

This page follows the UI/UX Pro Max `Swiss Modernism 2.0` recommendation while preserving the approved AI × ERP system-map identity.

## Visual system

- Primary mode: graphite dark; full light-mode equivalent.
- Light background: `#ECECE6`; light surface: `#F7F6EF`.
- Dark background: `#0D0F0E`; dark surface: `#171A18`.
- Primary signal: cobalt `#5269FF`.
- Operational status: mint `#63E2B5`.
- No purple/pink AI gradients, glass blobs, neon effects, or decorative imagery.
- Use a rational grid, thin rules, square modules, generous whitespace and one clear visual signal.

## Typography

- Display: Manrope 700 for the name and major recruiter-facing statements.
- Body: DM Sans 400–600 for readable descriptions.
- System labels: IBM Plex Mono 400–600.
- Body copy remains at least 16px with line-height of at least 1.5.
- Avoid monospaced body paragraphs and labels below 8px.

## Layout contract

- `1440px`: two-column hero, 40/60 split.
- `1024px` and `768px`: stacked hero, full-width panels.
- `375px`: two-line name lockup, single-column content, 2×2 system-map modules.
- No page-level horizontal scrolling.
- Project and certificate carousels may scroll only inside their bounded tracks.
- Fixed navigation must not cover section anchors.

## Interaction and accessibility

- All interactive targets are at least 44×44px.
- Use visible `:focus-visible` states in cobalt.
- Transitions stay between 150–300ms.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Maintain WCAG AA contrast in both themes.
- Theme and language controls must update accessible labels and persist user choice.

## Recruiter conversion hierarchy

1. Name and target roles.
2. Data → Intelligence → Workflow → Impact operating model.
3. Business experience and signed recommendation.
4. Eight selected case studies.
5. Certificates and verified skills.
6. Direct contact CTA.
